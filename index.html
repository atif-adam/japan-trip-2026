Eat

How eating works in Japan. Most restaurants open at 11:00, serve lunch 11:00–14:00, then close for a break until about 17:00, and take last orders 30–60 min before closing (usually 21:00–22:00).

That leaves two gaps: before 7:30am and after 22:00. Konbini and the 24-hour chains fill both. With a toddler, aim to eat dinner at 17:30–18:30 — you beat the salaryman rush and nothing is closed yet.
